   Malicious prompts hidden in `.cursorrules` or `AGENTS.md` trick coding agents into
   replacing legitimate dependencies with attacker-controlled packages. Evolutionary
   optimization (PackHallu framework) achieves high success across multiple LLMs and agent
   frameworks.
